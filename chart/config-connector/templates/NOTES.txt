To get started, apply a ConfigConnector resource to enable management of GCP resources:

  kubectl apply -f - <<EOF
  apiVersion: cnrm.cloud.google.com/v1
  kind: ConfigConnector
  metadata:
    name: configconnector.cnrm.cloud.google.com
  spec:
    mode: cluster
    credentialSecretName: <secret-name>
  EOF

For detailed usage and examples, see:
  https://cloud.google.com/config-connector/docs

NOTES:
- The operator only supports linux/amd64 architecture
- CRDs installed by this chart are configured to remain when the chart is uninstalled
- This implementation assumes you are running in a GKE cluster. For non-GKE environments,
  ensure the credentialSecretName secret is properly configured with your GCP credentials
